# EU/Europe Immigration Myth-Busting Statistics (compiled Aug 2026)

## 1. Perception gap: how much Europeans overestimate immigrant numbers

- **Myth**: Europeans believe immigrants make up a very large share of the population.
- **Reality (Ipsos Perils of Perception 2024 retrospective)**: multi-country average guess **24% of population are immigrants vs actual 11%** (14-country study). Average guess **28% of prisoners are immigrants vs actual 15%**. Overestimation of immigrant share is one of the most persistent misperceptions across a decade of research. [PDF](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/publication/documents/2025-01/ipsos-the-perils-of-perception-2024.pdf) (2024)
- **Special Eurobarometer on integration (2022)**: **68% overestimated** the share of migrants; Europeans believe on average **16% of population are non-EU citizens vs actual <7%**. [EC "Seven facts"](https://home-affairs.ec.europa.eu/news/seven-facts-help-understand-migration-2025-09-23_en) (2025)
- **Ipsos 2023 (via OWID)**: US perceived 33% vs 15%; Japan 10% vs ~2%. [OWID](https://ourworldindata.org/data-insights/many-people-overestimate-the-percentage-of-immigrants-in-their-country)
- Ipsos 2018: France guessed **31% of population Muslim vs actual 8%**. [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-uk/perils-perception-and-eu)
- **Flag**: per-country perceived-vs-actual pairs (Italy ~30% vs 10%, France ~29% vs 12%) are from the 2018 wave — not re-verified; 2024 PDF only carries the aggregate.

## 2. Irregular border crossings (Frontex detections) — post-2023 collapse

- 2015: **1.82M** (peak; detections, not persons — double counting)
- 2016: ~511k | 2017: ~205k | 2018: ~150k | 2019: ~142k | 2020: ~125k | 2021: ~200k | 2022: ~330k
- 2023: **~380,000** (highest since 2016)
- 2024: **239,000** (−38%)
- 2025: **~178,000** (−26%, lowest since 2021; less than half 2023)
- 2026 H1: **~49,000, −37% y/y** (Jan–Feb −52%; Q1 −40%)
- **2025 by route**: Central Med 66,328 (−1%, busiest, 37% of arrivals); Eastern Med −27%; W-African/Atlantic −63%; W-Balkans −42%; Belarus land border −37%; Western Med up (Algeria); Channel ~flat.
- **H1 2026 by route**: E-Med 16,600+ (−20%, busiest); C-Med 14,300 (−50%+); W-Med 7,900 (+17%, only major route rising); W-African 3,700 (−67%); Channel exit attempts 18,400 (−44%).
- Sources: [Frontex 2025 annual](https://www.frontex.europa.eu/media-centre/news/news-release/frontex-irregular-border-crossings-down-26-in-2025-europe-must-stay-prepared-lyKpVb) (Jan 2026); [H1 2026](https://www.frontex.europa.eu/media-centre/news/news-release/frontex-irregular-border-crossings-into-the-eu-down-37-in-the-first-half-of-2026-Z5YoLp) (Jul 2026); [2024](https://www.frontex.europa.eu/media-centre/news/news-release/irregular-border-crossings-into-eu-drop-sharply-in-2024-oqpweX); [EPRS briefing](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/BRIE/2025/767210/EPRS_BRI(2025)767210_EN.pdf) (2025).
- **Flags**: 2016–2021 totals from Frontex risk analyses, not re-verified digit-exact; H1 2026 preliminary.

## 3. Irregular arrivals are a small share of total EU immigration

- **Myth**: most migrants arrive "illegally" (47% of Italians believed more immigrants stay illegally than legally — Eurobarometer).
- **Reality**: EU issued **3.5M first residence permits to non-EU citizens in 2024** (employment 31.9%; family 27.1%; other incl. protection 25.3%; education 15.7%) vs 239k irregular crossings → irregular ≈ **6–7% of legal inflow**. EC: of ~4.1M who moved to the EU in 2023, **only ~10% were irregular**.
- EPRS: irregular entry ≠ irregular migration — **>75% of 2015 crossers were "likely refugees"** (obtained protection); ~55% for 2009–2021.
- Sources: [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20250912-2) (2025); EC Seven Facts; EPRS; [MPC on Eurobarometer](https://migrationpolicycentre.eu/special-eurobarometer-integration-immigrants-european-union-initial-reaction/).

## 4. Asylum applications 2015–2026 and the Assad effect

- EU+ (EUAA): 2015–16 ~1.3M/yr peak; 2021 ~648k; 2022 **966k**; 2023 **1.14M**; 2024 **~1.0M** (−11%); **2025 ~822k (−19%)**.
- **Syrian applications collapsed after Assad fell (8 Dec 2024)**: 2025 ~**42,000, −72% y/y**; monthly >16,000 (Oct 2024) → ~3,500 (Sep 2025). Syrians #1 → #3 (Venezuelans #1 by mid-2025). Germany lost top-receiver spot — France and Spain received more in 2025. 110k Syrian cases still pending end-Sep 2025.
- First-instance decisions down 18% in 2025 (Eurostat).
- Sources: [EUAA annual trends](https://www.euaa.europa.eu/latest-asylum-trends-annual-analysis) (2026); [EUAA Syria](https://www.euaa.europa.eu/news-events/syrian-asylum-applications-drop-significantly-reflecting-broader-decreasing-trend-eu); [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260424-1) (Apr 2026).
- **Flag**: 822k preliminary; 2021 ~648k not re-verified exactly.

## 5. Concentration: Germany/Austria outsized share; per-capita rankings

- **Germany: 150,500 protection grants in 2024 = 34% of EU total** (with ~19% of EU population). 2025: 103,360 = 29%; Germany+Spain+France = **70% of all EU grants** in 2025. German recognition rate 44.4% (2024) → 28.1% (2025) vs EU avg 39%.
- **Per-capita applications 2024**: Cyprus #1 (~7,200/million), Greece ~7,000/million. 2025 (Eurostat): Greece 5.3 first-time applicants per 1,000, then Cyprus & Spain (2.9), Luxembourg (2.6), Ireland (2.4). Frontline/small states top per-capita pressure, not Germany.
- **Austria**: 2023 second-highest per capita (6,500/million, 59k apps); highest positive decisions per capita on record 2023 (12,478 grants + ~5,500 subsidiary).
- Sources: [Eurostat asylum decisions](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Asylum_decisions_-_annual_statistics); [EUAA per-capita](https://www.euaa.europa.eu/asylum-report-2024/3141-asylum-applications-capita); [AIDA Germany](https://asylumineurope.org/reports/country/germany/statistics/); [EMN Austria 2024](https://www.emn.at/sites/default/files/2025/06/emn-austria-asylum-and-migration-overview-2024.pdf).

## 6. "Everyone comes to Europe" myth — who actually hosts refugees

- **UNHCR**: low/middle-income countries host **68% of the world's refugees**; **66% live in countries neighbouring their origin** (Mid-Year Trends 2025). Countries holding **27% of global wealth hosted 80% of refugees**.
- Global: **117.3M forcibly displaced end-June 2025** (−5% vs end-2024, returns to Syria/Afghanistan); ~42.5M refugees; 67.8M IDPs. Top hosts: Iran, Türkiye, Colombia, Germany, Uganda, Pakistan — mostly non-EU.
- Sources: [UNHCR Global Trends](https://www.unhcr.org/global-trends); [Mid-Year Trends 2025](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/2025-11/mid-year-trends-report-2025.pdf); [hosting metrics](https://www.unhcr.org/refugee-statistics/insights/explainers/refugee-hosting-metrics.html).
- **Flag**: verify whether 68% is from Global Trends 2024 (Jun 2025) or 2025 edition.

## 7. Mediterranean deaths (IOM Missing Migrants)

- **Cumulative: >30,000 dead/missing in the Mediterranean since 2014**; >72,000 all routes worldwide.
- By year (Med): 2015: 3,771; 2016: ~5,096 (deadliest); 2021: 2,048; 2022: 2,411; 2023: 3,155 (or 3,041 — conflicting IOM communications); 2024: 2,452; 2025: ~2,185; 2026 YTD (~Jul): ~1,300.
- 2024: deadliest year on record for migrants globally — **8,938 deaths**.
- Sources: [IOM Med region](https://missingmigrants.iom.int/region/mediterranean); [IOM 2024 release](https://www.iom.int/news/2024-deadliest-year-record-migrants-new-iom-data-reveals).
- **Flags**: 2023 toll conflicting (3,041 vs 3,155); 2025 from secondary reporting; 2015/16 partially confirmed.

## 8. Ukraine displacement dwarfs other flows

- **4.37M** non-EU citizens (overwhelmingly Ukrainians) under temporary protection in the EU as of 30 Apr 2026. Hosts: **Germany 1,229,960 (28.6%), Poland 965,005 (22.5%), Czechia 393,005 (9.1%)**.
- This single legal flow ≈ **25x** 2025's total irregular crossings (178k) and ~5x annual EU+ asylum applications — absorbed via a legal instrument (Temporary Protection Directive) without the predicted collapse.
- Sources: [Eurostat Jun 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260610-1); [Jul 2026](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260710-1).

## 9. Resettlement collapse to multi-decade lows

- **2025: ~37,000 UNHCR-supported resettlement departures, down from >116,000 in 2024** (−68%) — lowest quotas in two decades, below COVID-era. Vs **2.5M in need** (2026 estimate; 2025 ~2.9M).
- Driver: **US FY2026 ceiling cut to 7,500** — lowest in USRAP history (was 125,000).
- Sources: [UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/06/1167729) (Jun 2026); [UNHCR resettlement data](https://www.unhcr.org/us/what-we-do/build-better-futures/long-term-solutions/resettlement/resettlement-data); [USCRI](https://refugees.org/press-release-amid-record-global-displacement-u-s-lowers-refugee-admissions-to-historic-lows/).

## 10. Employment/welfare myths and demographics

- **Reality (Eurostat EU-LFS)**: non-EU citizen unemployment fell **21.4% (2014) → 12.3% (2024)** (nationals 5.1%). Immigrant (foreign-born) employment rate record **68.2% in 2025** (CReAM/Eurostat). Non-EU women part-time 36.8% vs 11.8% men (2024).
- **Demographics without migration (EUROPOP2025 via Bruegel)**: EU working-age population 264M → **207M by 2050 (−21%)** with zero immigration; total 451M → 406M; old-age dependency ratio **37.6% (2025) → 54.5% (2050)**.
- Sources: [Eurostat labour market integration](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Migrant_integration_statistics_%E2%80%93_labour_market_indicators); [CReAM report](https://www.rfberlin.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/RFBerlin_CReAM_Report072025_Employment-of-Migrants.pdf) (2025); [Bruegel](https://www.bruegel.org/policy-brief/how-demographic-change-will-hit-debt-sustainability-european-union-countries); [Eurostat projections](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Population_projections_in_the_EU).
- **Flag**: 68.2% is foreign-born (CReAM definition); Eurostat non-EU-citizen employment rate is ~62–63% — don't conflate.

## Cross-cutting flags

- All 2025/2026 Frontex and EUAA figures preliminary, routinely revised.
- Frontex counts detections, not unique persons — double counting inflates totals (esp. 2015–16).
- Conflicts noted: 2023 Med deaths; 2018-wave per-country perception pairs.
