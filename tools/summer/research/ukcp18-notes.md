## UK-wide

- Growing season: England 2006-2015 averaged 280 days, 29 days longer than
  1961-1990 (5C-based definition; last published ~2016, cite with year caveat).
  State of UK Climate 2024: +21% growing degree days 2015-2024 vs 1961-1990.
- Observed extremes (SoC 2023): 2014-2023 vs 1961-1990: days >=25C +63%,
  >=28C more than doubled, >=30C more than trebled. SoC 2025: London days
  >30C and nights >18C more than quadrupled (2016-2025 vs 1961-1990).
- UKCP18 headline precip (2070, RCP8.5, 10th-90th): summer -45% to +5%,
  winter -3% to +39%. Hot spells (>=30C 2+ days): 0.20 -> 4.1 per year by 2070s.

## Essay implications

- All 8 picker cities have consistent hot-day/tropical-night data: dot
  calendar section fully supported.
- Tropical nights: real story only for London (and 4C Manchester/Cardiff);
  for Edinburgh/Glasgow/Belfast/Leeds say "projected to remain rare".
- Cardiff record: soften or use Wales record clearly labelled not-Cardiff.
- Frame projections as "a 2C world (2031-2056)" and "a 4C world (late
  century)" rather than fake-precise years; disclose RCP8.5 UKCP18 Local and
  that Bastin analogs are RCP4.5 CMIP5 (different scenario).
